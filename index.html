How it works

The standings are rebuilt after every game goes final. Every number below is worked out from the games played so far, so a team is compared with where it should be today, not with where it should finish in April.

W, L and Pct
Wins, losses and winning percentage (wins divided by games played). Each conference is ranked by Pct. Teams on the same Pct are split by their winning percentage in the games among just those teams (when each of them has played at least one of the others), then by point differential per game, then by name. A team that has not played yet is not ranked.
vs. Vegas
The Vegas number is each team’s preseason win total, frozen on Oct. 17, 2026, before opening night, and never updated during the season. vs. Vegas is how far the team’s winning percentage is above or below the one that win total stands for, in percentage points. A 49.5-win line is 49.5 of 82 games, or 60.4%, so a team at 1-0 (100%) is +39.6% and a team at 10-10 (50%) is −10.4%.
The Vegas lines
Consensus win totals from six sportsbooks, compiled by Boyd’s Bets and frozen as of Oct. 17, 2026, just before opening night. They never change during the season. The 30 lines add up to 1,237 wins, but an 82-game season only has 1,230 to hand out (every game has one winner), so the books were 7 wins too generous across the league, about a quarter of a win per team.
vs. Last Year
The same comparison with the team’s 2025-26 record instead of the line: 45 wins last season is 45 of 82, or 54.9%, so a team at 1-0 now is +45.1%.
$ per Win
The team’s full 2026-27 payroll (every salary on its books in Salary Season Finder, including its share of players paid by more than one team) divided by its wins so far. It starts high and comes down with every win: a $210 million payroll is $210.0M per win after one win and $5.0M after 42. Lower is better. A team without a win shows a dash. The team pages’ $ per Win charts and the As of and Since views use the payroll saved with each night (for Since, the night you pick).
Pt Diff
Average scoring margin per game: points scored minus points allowed, divided by games played.
Exp. W-L
The record a team’s scoring suggests, using the Pythagorean formula with exponent 14: expected wins = games × PF¹⁴ / (PF¹⁴ + PA¹⁴), rounded to whole wins.
Luck
Actual wins minus expected wins. A positive number means the team has won more than its point differential says it should, usually by winning close games.
Road W − Home L
Road wins minus home losses. The old rule of thumb for a contender is to win at home and steal some on the road; a positive number means the road wins are outpacing the home slip-ups.
Last 10
The team’s record over its last 10 games, or all of them if it has played fewer.
vs. .500+
The team’s record against teams that are at .500 or better now (for As of, as they stood that night).
Biggest movers
The teams whose vs. Vegas number changed most since the standings saved after the previous night of games.
Timeframes
Season shows the whole season. The standings are saved once every game of a night is final, and you can look back from any saved night. As of shows the standings exactly as they were that night. Since shows only the games played after that night: wins, losses, points and home and road records are the current ones minus that night’s, and every column is worked out again from those, with the full payroll divided by the wins in the window. Last 10 and vs. .500+ use the games in the view. Last 7 days and Last 15 days count back from the latest night with games. If a night was not saved, the nearest earlier one is used and the page says so.
