Payroll vs. win percentage

Dashed lines: .500 and league-average payroll

How it works

The standings are rebuilt after every game goes final. Every number below is worked out from the games played so far, so a team is compared with where it should be today, not with where it should finish in April.

W, L and Pct
Wins, losses and winning percentage (wins divided by games played). Each conference is ranked by Pct, with more wins breaking ties.
vs. Vegas
Wins above or below the pace of the team’s preseason win total. A team with a 41.5-win line is expected to win 41.5 of 82 games, so after 20 games it should have about 10.1 wins. At 12-8 it is +1.9; at 8-12 it is −2.1.
The Vegas lines
Consensus win totals from six sportsbooks, compiled by Boyd’s Bets and frozen as of Oct. 17, 2026, just before opening night. They never change during the season. The 30 lines add up to 1,237 wins, but an 82-game season only has 1,230 to hand out (every game has one winner), so the books were 7 wins too generous across the league, about a quarter of a win per team.
vs. Last Year
The same pace math, using each team’s 2025-26 win total instead of the line.
$ per Win
The team’s 2026-27 payroll (every salary on its books in Salary Season Finder, including its share of players paid by more than one team), prorated to the games played and divided by wins. Lower is better. A team without a win shows a dash. The team pages’ $ per Win charts and the As of and Since views use the payroll saved with each night (for Since, the night you pick).
Pt Diff
Average scoring margin per game: points scored minus points allowed, divided by games played.
Exp. W-L
The record a team’s scoring suggests, using the Pythagorean formula with exponent 14: expected wins = games × PF¹⁴ / (PF¹⁴ + PA¹⁴), rounded to whole wins.
Luck
Actual wins minus expected wins. A positive number means the team has won more than its point differential says it should, usually by winning close games.
Road W − Home L
Road wins minus home losses. The old rule of thumb for a contender is to win at home and steal some on the road; a positive number means the road wins are outpacing the home slip-ups.
Biggest movers
The teams whose vs. Vegas number changed most since the standings saved after the previous night of games.
Timeframes
Season shows the whole season. The standings are saved once every game of a night is final, and you can look back from any saved night. As of shows the standings exactly as they were that night. Since shows only the games played after that night: wins, losses, points and home and road records are the current ones minus that night’s, and every column is worked out again from those, with payroll prorated to the games in the window. Last 7 days and Last 15 days count back from the latest night with games. If a night was not saved, the nearest earlier one is used and the page says so.

Sources: records, points scored and allowed, and home and road records from ESPN’s standings feed. Payroll from Salary Season Finder. Before a team’s first game, and wherever a number cannot be worked out yet, the table shows a dash.
